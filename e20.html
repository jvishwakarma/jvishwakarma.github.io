THE CHALLENGE

The traditional friction of SME onboarding

Opening a corporate bank account in the UAE was a cumbersome, paper-heavy process that held back business agility.

Entrepreneurs had to schedule multiple branch visits and bring physical trade licences, original Emirates IDs, passport copies and complicated Memorandum of Association files. Compliance checks navigated multiple government systems by hand, dragging the process out for weeks.

Key pain points identified

  1. 01In-person identity verification at physical branches
  2. 02Complex legal documentation for multi-director setups
  3. 03Prolonged KYC and AML evaluation times
  4. 04Opaque status tracking with zero customer visibility
THE SOLUTION

Fully mobile onboarding in under 10 minutes

From eligibility checks and document scans to package selection and remote signature capture. Secure integrations with UAE PASS and regulatory departments let owners submit verified corporate credentials straight from their phone, while progress tracking and smart validation cut drop-offs.

Digital KYC & verification

Automated validation of trade licences, board resolutions and partner identities against real-time regulatory databases.

Tailored banking packages

Side-by-side comparison screens to choose a banking structure that fits expected transaction volumes.

Smart onboarding flow

A step-by-step wizard that saves drafts, so owners can pause and resume while gathering documents.

Biometric authentication

On-device face scan binds security keys to authorised account signatories, alongside NFC passport and Emirates ID checks.
