E20 launch campaign

A self-initiated launch campaign for E20, the business bank I designed. One idea, a business account in 10 minutes, carried from the product UI into a launch film, social cuts, feature graphics and a landing-page hero.

ROLE
Art direction, design, motion
PRODUCT
E20 Digital Business Bank
TOOLS
Figma, After Effects, Premiere
OUTPUTS
1 film · 3 social cuts · 3 statics · 1 web hero
Launch film30–45 seconds, sound on. The 10-minute journey told through the product UI, cut on action.
THE BRIEF

One promise, everywhere it shows up.

GOAL Make a regulated, paperwork-heavy process feel instant and effortless, without losing trust.
AUDIENCE Founders, freelancers and SME owners in the UAE who have lost days to branch visits.
MESSAGE “Open a business account in 10 minutes.” Every asset proves it with the real product, not a metaphor.
ART DIRECTION

Built from the brand system, not around it.

Every asset uses E20's existing colour, type and components. The UI is the hero: it stays legible in every frame, and motion only ever explains what the product is doing.

Colour
E20 red carries action and progress only. Everything else is ink and paper, so the product reads first.
Type Aa Aa Plus Jakarta Sans for the promise and the proof; Instrument Serif italic for the one word that matters.
Motion
  • One easing curve, one spring, across every asset
  • UI never moves faster than it can be read
  • Cut on action: a tap starts the next scene
  • Loops resolve cleanly for social autoplay
SOCIAL CUTS

Three formats, one story.

Each cut is built for its feed: hooks land in the first second, captions carry the story with sound off, and every loop ends where it began.

Stories & Reels · 9:16The onboarding steps ticking off in real time.
Feed · 4:5Card, IBAN and invoicing fanning out from one account.
LinkedIn · 1:1The headline result, as a stat-led loop.
FEATURE GRAPHICS

Statics that prove the claim.

A set of launch-day statics for the website, App Store and press kit. Each pairs one real number with the piece of UI that earned it.

ACCOUNT OPENING 10 min Account setup: 100%
COMPLETION RATE 85% Trade licence ✓ Emirates ID ✓ Account live
LOVED BY MERCHANTS 4.8 ★ App Store rating from 10,000+ businesses opened on E20.

A landing hero that moves like the product.

The hero composition for the launch page, with a short UI sequence that shows the first tap of onboarding instead of describing it.

Landing page heroA six-second loop: tap, verify, continue.
